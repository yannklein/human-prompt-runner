What this is not

This is not SEO, and it is not a marketing analytics tool. It does not attempt to “optimize” your company. It reveals how your public signals are interpreted and where interpretation breaks.

  • Not a traffic or keyword tool
  • Not a generic AI copilot
  • Not prescriptive “growth hacks”
  • Not a judgment of internal quality

How it works (at the right level)

We synthesize publicly available signals — publications, patents, team profiles, press, and investor disclosures — through a structured evaluation framework designed to mirror investor reasoning. The platform is intentionally constrained: if a signal is not visible publicly, it is not assumed.

Important distinction

We do not score your company’s true quality. We score the signals your company emits and how they are interpreted by AI systems upstream of investor decision-making.

FAQs

Is this just another way to say SEO?

No. SEO optimizes for ranking and traffic. This evaluates credibility signals and how AI systems interpret them. If SEO asks “Are you visible?”, we ask “Are you believed?”.

Are you telling us how investable we actually are?

No. We do not assess internal quality. We assess how public signals are interpreted. Many strong companies score lower because their strengths are not legible in public information.

What sources do you use?

Public information only by default: websites, team profiles, publications/citations, patents, press, and investor disclosures. We do not require pitch decks or internal documents for a baseline assessment.

What happens when we ask “what can we do about this”?

We prioritize the small number of missing signals that matter most for your next milestone (e.g., fundraising, senior hiring, partnerships). We intentionally avoid tactical checklists. The product is designed to deliver interpretation and prioritization.

How often should we reassess?

Most companies reassess quarterly during active narrative shifts or before/after major announcements. Perception changes slowly — and that stability is useful.
